# Master Website Audit v2.0 — Read-Only Audit Plan

The uploaded brief asks for an independent, agency-grade audit of digitalymarket.com, with **no changes to the website** until you approve findings. This plan covers producing that audit only.

## What will be done
1. **Inventory (Stage 1)** — every reachable page, navigation, header/footer, sections, forms, buttons, plus the site's building blocks, fonts, colours, images, logos, case studies and connected services (enquiry form, Google Sheet, Google tag). Each item: location, purpose, condition, reusability, risk.
2. **Full page coverage** — every page in the sitemap visited on desktop and phone, with screenshots; any page not checked is listed as a gap.
3. **Test each of your hypotheses** (generic look, text-heavy, weak storytelling, founder story, business understanding, service presentation, hierarchy and enquiry journeys) — each marked confirmed, partly confirmed or not confirmed, with evidence.
4. **Specialist reviews** — brand and messaging, design, user experience, conversion, search visibility, speed (only measured numbers, no invented scores), accessibility, tracking, technical quality, content claims (e.g. SensyTalk figures).
5. **Enquiry form check** — tested without sending real enquiries to you; anything not safely testable is reported as a limitation.
6. **Competitor comparison** — 2–3 relevant agencies, used for ideas only, never copied.
7. **Redesign strategy** — Preserve / Improve / Redesign / Remove / Introduce, with impact and effort scored separately; brand-kit direction suggested but not finalised.
8. **Prioritised action plan** — phases, first sprint, and a "devil's advocate" review of the recommendations, plus a clear list of what could not be inspected.

## Deliverable
- A downloadable audit report saved to your Files, with a short summary in chat.
- Nothing on the website, database, Google accounts or domain is changed. Work stops after delivery and waits for your approval.
