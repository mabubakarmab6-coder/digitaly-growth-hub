# DigitalyMarket Content Bible

## Core message

DigitalyMarket helps businesses turn their digital presence into measurable business growth.

## Content rules

- Lead with the business problem, not the tool.
- Explain capabilities in human language.
- Keep claims specific and supportable.
- Prefer practical examples over vague promises.
- Never invent client proof or statistics.
- Distinguish founder experience from agency client work.
- Keep the audience broad enough for small businesses while retaining B2B credibility.

## Website content hierarchy

### Homepage
The homepage should answer, in order:
1. What is DigitalyMarket?
2. Who does it help?
3. What can it do?
4. Why should I trust it?
5. How does the engagement work?
6. What should I do next?

### Services
The services overview should explain the four capabilities and outcomes. Detailed service pages should only be created when each service has enough useful, truthful content to justify its own page.

### Founder
Founder content should demonstrate business experience and strategic perspective without implying that previous employers were DigitalyMarket clients.

### Work
Until real agency projects are available, the site should use an intentional "next chapter is being built" message rather than fake portfolio entries.

### Insights
Initial content pillars:
- GEO
- Paid Marketing
- E-commerce
- Digital Growth
- Web & Conversion
- Business Growth

Articles should be written for real business questions, not keyword stuffing.

## Proof framework

Current proof foundation:
- founder experience
- practical operating experience
- e-commerce and marketplace exposure
- sales/business development experience
- operations/CRM experience

Future proof:
- documented agency projects
- real before/after improvements
- client testimonials with permission
- verified case studies

## Founder messaging

Core positioning: **Founder & Growth Strategist**.

Message direction: Abubakar's career crossed sales, e-commerce, marketplaces, operations, business development and entrepreneurship; DigitalyMarket brings those experiences into a modern digital growth approach.

## Content quality checklist

Before publishing any page/article:
- Is the audience clear?
- Is the business problem clear?
- Is the claim supportable?
- Does the content sound human?
- Does it provide actual value?
- Does it have a useful next step?
- Does it accidentally imply fake client work?
- Does it support the brand voice?
